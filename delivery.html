FAQ

Questions?

Will my standard insurance really drop me for doing DoorDash?
Yes. Almost all standard personal auto policies strictly prohibit using your car for "livery" or commercial delivery. If you get into an accident while working, they will deny the claim and likely cancel your policy immediately.
What is the "Period 1 Coverage Gap"?
When you turn on the Uber or Lyft app but haven't accepted a ride yet (Period 1), your personal insurance stops covering you, but the app's commercial insurance hasn't kicked in yet. A Rideshare endorsement closes this dangerous legal gap.
Do I need a full commercial policy?
Usually, no. Full commercial policies are for fleets and heavy trucks, and they cost thousands of dollars. We secure "hybrid" or endorsed policies that let you legally drive for apps for just a fraction of a commercial premium.
How fast can I upload my insurance to the app?
Instantly. Once your policy is active, you can download your Declarations Page right from our portal and upload it straight to your driver app so you can start earning money today.
