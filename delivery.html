FAQ

Questions?

Will my standard insurance really drop me for doing DoorDash?
Yes. Almost all standard personal auto policies strictly prohibit using your car for "livery" or commercial delivery. If you get into an accident while working, they will deny the claim and very likely cancel your policy immediately.
What is the "Period 1 Coverage Gap"?
When you turn on the Uber or Lyft app but haven't accepted a ride yet (Period 1), your personal insurance stops covering you, but the app's commercial insurance hasn't kicked in yet. A rideshare endorsement closes this dangerous legal gap — it's the most important thing a gig driver can have.
Do I need a full commercial policy?
Usually no. Full commercial policies are for fleets and heavy trucks — they cost thousands per year. We secure hybrid or endorsed policies that let you legally drive for apps for a fraction of the commercial premium through Warrior Insurance.
How fast can I upload my insurance to the app?
Instantly. Once your policy is active you download your Declarations Page right from our portal and upload it straight to your driver app — formatted exactly how Uber, Lyft, and DoorDash require. You can start earning the same day.
I drive for multiple apps. Can one policy cover all of them?
Yes. Warrior's policies are designed specifically for multi-app gig drivers. Whether you switch between Uber, DoorDash, and Amazon Flex in the same shift — one policy covers all of it.
I have a bad driving record. Can you still help?
Yes. Warrior specializes in non-standard risk and accepts drivers that other carriers reject — including those needing SR-22 filings. We typically get same-day SR-22 processing so you're back on the road fast.
