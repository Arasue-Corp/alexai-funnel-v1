What Your Policy Covers

No fine print surprises. Know exactly what protects your home before you sign anything.

Dwelling (Structure)

Your home's walls, roof, floors, built-in appliances — covered for damage from fire, wind, hail, and more.

Personal Property

Furniture, electronics, clothing, appliances — your belongings are protected inside your home and even when traveling.

Liability Protection

Someone gets hurt on your property and sues. We cover legal costs, medical bills, and settlements up to your policy limit.

Loss of Use (ALE)

If your home becomes uninhabitable, we pay for hotel, meals, and temporary housing until you're back home.

Fire & Smoke Damage

Arizona's desert dry season makes fire risk real. Structure, personal property, and cleanup are all fully covered.

Wind & Hail Damage

Monsoon season brings serious roof damage. Hail, windstorm, and fallen tree damage are covered under standard policies.

Flood Damage

Standard policies do NOT cover flooding — including Arizona monsoon flash floods. A separate flood policy is required and strongly recommended.

Earthquake Damage

Earthquakes require a separate policy. Arizona has moderate seismic activity — worth considering, especially near fault lines.

Normal Wear & Tear

Aging roof, old plumbing, or an HVAC that finally gave out — maintenance issues are the homeowner's responsibility.

Vacant / Unoccupied Homes

If your home is vacant for 60+ days, coverage may be suspended. Vacant home policies exist specifically for this scenario.

Sewer / Drain Backup

Water backing up from sewers or drains is typically excluded. A low-cost endorsement can add this — we'll advise if your home needs it.

Home Business Liability

Running a business from home? Inventory, equipment, and client liability require separate commercial coverage.

Flood Insurance

Flash floods during monsoon season are Arizona's #1 uninsured home risk. We add a dedicated flood policy — NFIP or private carriers — starting around $600/yr.

Highly Recommended for AZ

Solar Panel Coverage

Arizona has the highest solar adoption rate in the US. Many standard policies undervalue rooftop panels — we get the right replacement cost documented.

Essential for AZ Solar Homes

Extended Replacement Cost

If a major monsoon drives up construction costs, standard coverage may fall short. Extended replacement cost covers 25–50% above your dwelling limit.

Scheduled Personal Property

Jewelry, art, and collectibles often exceed standard limits. We schedule them individually so each piece is insured at its full appraised value.

Equipment Breakdown

AC units and water heaters failing from mechanical breakdown aren't covered by default. In Arizona's 115°F summers, this endorsement pays for itself fast.

Critical in AZ Heat

Personal Umbrella Policy

If a liability claim exceeds your home limit, an umbrella kicks in. $1M in extra coverage typically costs under $20/month — incredible value.

FAQ

Questions?

Can I bundle my home and auto insurance?
Absolutely. Bundling is one of the easiest ways to save. Our advisors compare rates to see if bundling with one carrier, or splitting them between two specialists, gives you the better overall value.
My insurance is paid through my mortgage escrow. Is switching hard?
Not at all. We handle 100% of the heavy lifting. We contact your lender, send them the new policy declarations, request payment from your escrow account, and help cancel your old policy. You don't have to lift a finger.
Are your advisors actually real people?
Yes! While we use technology to speed up the quoting process, home insurance is too important to leave purely to a bot. You will have a dedicated, licensed Arizona advisor to review your assets and answer your questions.
How are you able to charge $0 in agency fees?
We removed the expensive overhead of traditional agencies — no strip-mall offices, no paper files, no legacy software. By running an efficient digital infrastructure, we pass those savings directly to you.
Does my home need Arizona-specific coverage?
Absolutely. Haboobs, monsoons, extreme heat, and flash flooding create risks that generic out-of-state policies often underestimate. We always recommend flood and equipment breakdown endorsements for local homeowners.
How soon can I get covered?
In most cases same day or next business day. Submit the form, your advisor will call you within hours, and we can bind a policy immediately once you're ready.
