FAQ

Questions?

Can I bundle my home and auto insurance?
Absolutely. In fact, bundling is one of the easiest ways to save. Our advisors will compare rates to see if bundling with one carrier, or splitting them between two specialists, gives you the better overall value.
My current insurance is paid through my mortgage escrow. Is switching hard?
Not at all. We handle 100% of the heavy lifting. We will contact your lender, send them the new policy declarations, request the payment from your escrow account, and even help cancel your old policy. You don't have to lift a finger.
Are your advisors actually real people?
Yes! While we use advanced technology to speed up the quoting process, home insurance is too important to leave purely to a bot. You will have a dedicated, licensed Arizona advisor to review your assets and answer your calls.
How are you able to charge $0 in agency fees?
We removed the expensive overhead of traditional agencies (strip-mall offices, paper files, outdated legacy software). By running a highly efficient digital infrastructure, we can afford to pass those savings directly to you.
