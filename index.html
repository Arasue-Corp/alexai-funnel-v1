What You're Getting

What's Covered — and What's Not

No surprises. We believe you should know exactly what protects you before you commit.

Collision Coverage

Damage to your car from accidents — whether it's your fault or not. Fender bender, total loss, we've got it.

Comprehensive Coverage

Theft, vandalism, fire, flooding, hailstorms, and even hitting an animal. Arizona weather is covered.

Bodily Injury & Liability

Covers medical and legal costs if you injure someone. Required by Arizona law — and we make sure you're fully compliant.

Uninsured Motorist

1 in 5 Arizona drivers has no insurance. We make sure you're protected when they're not.

Roadside Assistance

Flat tire, dead battery, lockout, or you're out of gas — we dispatch help anywhere in Arizona, 24/7.

Rental Car Reimbursement

Your car is in the shop? We cover your rental so you're never stranded without wheels.

Intentional Damage

If you deliberately damage your own vehicle or someone else's property, your policy won't cover it.

Racing or Stunts

Using your car in speed contests, drag racing, or stunt driving voids your coverage during that activity.

Business / Rideshare Use

Driving for Uber, Lyft, or commercial purposes requires commercial coverage — standard personal policies don't cover this.

Mechanical Breakdown

Normal wear & tear, engine failure, or manufacturer defects aren't covered. That's what warranties are for.

Unlisted Vehicles

Coverage only applies to vehicles explicitly listed on your policy. Drive a second car? Add it first.

DUI / Impaired Driving

Claims arising from driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs can be denied under your policy terms.
