FAQ

Questions?

What is a BOP and do I need one?
A Business Owner's Policy (BOP) combines General Liability and Commercial Property insurance into one package. It is usually the most cost-effective way for small to medium businesses to get broad protection against lawsuits, injuries, and property damage.
Do you handle Workers Compensation in Arizona?
Yes. We work with carriers like Employers and biBerk to provide fast, compliant Workers Compensation policies that protect your employees and meet all Arizona state legal requirements.
How fast can I get a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for a job?
Once your policy is bound, you can generate and email COIs instantly through our digital client portal, 24/7. You don't have to wait for an agent to open their office to secure your next contract.
Do you cover independent contractors and freelancers?
Absolutely. Whether you are an IT consultant needing Professional Liability (E&O), an artisan contractor needing General Liability, or a real estate investor managing properties, our massive network of 42+ carriers has a tailored solution for your specific risk.
