FAQ

Questions?

What is a BOP and do I need one?
A Business Owner's Policy (BOP) combines General Liability and Commercial Property insurance into one package. It's the most cost-effective way for small to medium businesses to get broad protection against lawsuits, injuries, and property damage. Most businesses with a physical location or client-facing operations need one.
Do you handle Workers Compensation in Arizona?
Yes. Arizona requires Workers Comp for any business with at least one employee. We work with carriers like Employers and biBerk to provide fast, compliant policies. Penalties for non-compliance in Arizona can be severe, so we prioritize getting you covered quickly.
How fast can I get a Certificate of Insurance (COI) for a job?
Once your policy is bound, you generate COIs yourself — instantly, 24/7, directly from our client portal. No waiting for an agent to open their office, no calling during business hours. You can email them directly to general contractors or clients in seconds.
Do you cover independent contractors and freelancers?
Absolutely. Whether you're an IT consultant needing Professional Liability (E&O), an artisan contractor needing General Liability, or a real estate investor managing properties, our network of 42+ carriers has a tailored solution for your specific risk profile.
My business is in a specialized or high-risk industry. Can you still help?
Yes — this is where our 42+ carrier network really shines. Specialty markets like Palomar, Pathpoint, and SES Risk Solutions handle risks that standard carriers decline. Older buildings, food trucks, cannabis-adjacent businesses, hard-to-place industries — bring them to us.
What does the advisor consultation cost?
Nothing. The consultation is completely free, and as long as you bind your policy through our digital platform, there are no broker fees ever. Our advisors are paid by carriers, not by you — which is why they can act as true fiduciaries focused on your best interest.
