FAQ

Questions?

Why does my landlord require renters insurance?
Your landlord's insurance only covers the physical building. Renters insurance covers YOUR personal belongings inside the apartment, and provides liability coverage in case someone gets hurt inside your unit or if you accidentally cause damage (like a kitchen fire or bathtub leak).
Does it cover my laptop if it's stolen from my car?
Yes! Renters insurance typically includes "Off-Premises" coverage. This means your personal items (laptop, phone, bicycle) are protected against theft even when you are traveling, at a coffee shop, or if they are stolen out of your vehicle.
How fast can I get the proof for my move-in?
Instantly. As soon as you complete the 90-second checkout, your Declarations Page (Proof of Insurance) is generated. You can download it as a PDF immediately and we can auto-email it directly to your property manager.
Can I add my roommate to the policy?
Yes, you can easily add a roommate or a partner to your policy during the quote process or later through the app, ensuring both of your belongings are fully protected.
