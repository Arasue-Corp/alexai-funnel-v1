What Renters Insurance Actually Covers

Most people have no idea how much a renters policy actually protects. Here's the real breakdown.

Tech & Electronics

Laptop, phone, TV, gaming setup — covered against theft, fire, and accidental damage. Even away from home.

Furniture & Belongings

Everything inside your apartment — couch, clothes, kitchen gear, mattress. Covered against fire, smoke, theft, and water damage.

Temporary Housing (ALE)

If your apartment becomes uninhabitable after a fire or covered event, we cover hotel, meals, and temporary housing.

Liability Protection

Accidentally flood your downstairs neighbor? Someone trips in your unit? We cover legal costs and damages up to $100K+.

Theft Off-Premises

Laptop stolen at a cafe? Bike taken from outside the gym? Your stuff is protected even when it's not in your apartment.

Pet Liability

Your dog bites someone at the park. Select carriers cover pet-related liability so you're protected from claims.

Flood Damage

Standard renters policies exclude flooding. Arizona monsoons can flood ground-floor units — ask about a separate flood endorsement.

Roommate's Stuff

Unless your roommate is listed on the policy, their belongings are NOT covered. They need their own policy — or add them in the app.

Building Damage

Your landlord's policy covers the building. Renters insurance covers only YOUR property and liability — not the structure or shared areas.

Business Equipment

High-value business gear above your policy sub-limit may require a separate inland marine or business owners policy.

Intentional Damage

Deliberate damage to your apartment or a neighbor's property isn't covered. Coverage applies to accidents and unexpected events only.

Motor Vehicles

Your car or motorcycle needs its own auto policy. Bicycles and e-bikes are typically covered by renters insurance though.

FAQ

Questions?

Why does my landlord require renters insurance?
Your landlord's insurance only covers the building. Renters insurance covers YOUR belongings and provides liability coverage if someone gets hurt in your unit or if you accidentally cause damage like a kitchen fire or bathtub overflow.
Does it cover my laptop if it's stolen from my car?
Yes! Renters insurance includes "Off-Premises" coverage. Your personal items — laptop, phone, bicycle — are protected against theft even when at a coffee shop, gym, or stolen from your vehicle.
How fast can I get proof for my move-in?
Instantly. As soon as you complete the 90-second checkout, your Declarations Page is generated. Download it as a PDF and we auto-email it directly to your property manager — they get it before you've closed your laptop.
Can I add my roommate to the policy?
Yes. Add a roommate or partner during the quote process or anytime from the app. Their belongings and liability are covered under the same policy — no new quote required.
What if I need to cancel or change my policy?
100% digital — change your address, coverage limits, or cancel anytime from the app. Moving to a new apartment? Update your address in seconds and your new landlord gets updated proof automatically.
Is $14/month really the real price?
